Employment Income

Where to find it
FED
T4 Box 14 — Total employment income. Add all T4s if multiple jobs.
QC
RL-1 Box A — Revenus d'emploi (Québec residents only)
Effect on tax
100% taxable. Your employer withheld income tax, CPP/QPP, and EI each pay period. Box 14 is gross income before those deductions.
Math
Tax = (Income − BPA − deductions) × bracket rates. Federal BPA 2025: $16,129. QC BPA: $17,183.
T4 Box 14 — gross salary before any deductions

Effect on tax
SE income is taxed + you pay both halves of CPP/QPP (~11.9% on net). Half is deductible, half gives a credit. Switch to Advanced for full expense breakdown.

Investment Income

Where to find it
FED
T5 — Interest (Box 13), Eligible dividends (Box 24), Non-eligible (Box 10)
QC
RL-3 — Investment income Québec slip
Effect on tax
Interest = 100% taxable. Eligible dividends get a gross-up + credit. Capital gains: 50% inclusion rate. TFSA income is always tax-free — exclude it here.
Interest + dividends + capital gains — exclude TFSA (always tax-free)

Other Taxable Income

Includes
RRSP withdrawals (T4RSP Box 22), pension income (T4A Box 16), EI benefits (T4E Box 14), CERB, rental income, alimony received.
Effect on tax
Generally 100% taxable at your marginal rate. Eligible pension income qualifies for the Pension Income Credit (up to $2,000 federal).
RRSP withdrawals, EI benefits, pension income, rental income

RRSP — How it saves you tax

Where to find it
FED
RRSP contribution receipt from your bank. Deadline: March 3, 2026.
NOTE
Check My CRA Account → RRSP Deduction Limit for your exact room.
Effect on tax
Reduces taxable income dollar for dollar. At 36% marginal rate, $10,000 RRSP = $3,600 less combined tax. Withdrawal taxed as income later (usually lower rate).
2025 limit
Max $32,490 (18% of 2024 earned income) plus unused room from prior years.
Max $32,490 · Deadline Mar 3, 2026
✕

FHSA — First Home Savings Account

Why it's powerful
Deductible like RRSP AND tax-free on withdrawal like TFSA for your first home. $8,000/year, lifetime max $40,000. Best of both worlds.
Where to find it
FED
RC720 slip from your financial institution. On T1 Schedule 15.
Max $8,000/yr · Deductible now + tax-free for first home

Other Deductions

Includes
Union/professional dues (T4 Box 44), childcare (T778), moving (T1-M), student loan interest, employment expenses (T2200 required), carrying charges on investments.
Effect on tax
All reduce taxable income dollar-for-dollar. Each $1,000 saves you marginal rate × $1,000. Switch to Advanced for individual fields.
Union dues, childcare, moving, professional fees, student loan interest

Federal Tax Withheld

Where to find it
FED
T4 Box 22 — Income tax deducted. Add all T4s if multiple jobs.
Math
Federal balance = federal tax owing − this amount. Negative = CRA refunds you. Extra income (SE, investments) may mean you owe more.
T4 Box 22 — income tax remitted to CRA by employer

Provincial Tax Withheld

Where to find it (QC)
PROV
RL-1 Box E — Impôt du Québec retenu. Separate from federal.
Math
Provincial balance = provincial tax owing − this amount. For QC residents this is filed separately with Revenu Québec.
RL-1 Box E — Québec income tax withheld
