RRSP — How it saves you tax

Where to find it
FED
RRSP contribution receipt from your bank/broker. Deadline: March 3, 2026 for 2025 tax year.
NOTE
Check My CRA Account → RRSP/PRPP Deduction Limit for your exact room.
Effect on tax
Reduces taxable income dollar for dollar. If your marginal rate is 36%, $10,000 RRSP contribution = $3,600 less in combined tax. Withdrawal is taxed as income (usually at lower retirement rate).
2025 limit
Max $32,490 (18% of 2024 earned income). Plus unused room from prior years.
Max $32,490 · Check My CRA Account for your exact room · Deadline Mar 3, 2026

FHSA — First Home Savings Account

Where to find it
FED
RC720 slip from your financial institution. Contributions are on T1 Schedule 15.
Why it's powerful
Deductible like RRSP AND tax-free withdrawal like TFSA for your first home. $8,000/year room, lifetime max $40,000. Best of both worlds. Opens at any major bank — takes 10 minutes.
Who qualifies
First-time buyer = you have not owned a home in the current year or any of the preceding 4 years. Must be 18+, Canadian resident.
Max $8,000/yr · Deductible now + tax-free for first home
